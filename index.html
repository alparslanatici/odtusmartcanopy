ODTÜ Solar Canopy & Smart Charging Initiative

Transforming vast parking lots and ring stations into renewable energy hubs for EVs, e-bikes, and the student body.

Explore the Solution

The Campus Problem

Unutilized Space

ODTÜ's vast parking lots and open spaces currently absorb heat and offer zero utility beyond vehicle storage.

Lack of Charging Infrastructure

As electric vehicles and e-scooters become more common, the campus lacks the centralized grid support to charge them efficiently.

Student Connectivity

Students waiting at ring stations have no access to mobile device charging, limiting productivity between classes.

The Smart Solar Solution

Renewable Energy Generation

By installing high-efficiency solar canopies over existing parking lots, we generate clean kW/h directly fed into the campus micro-grid.

Integrated Mobility

The energy powers designated EV charging stations and e-scooter hubs, creating a sustainable transport loop.

Smart Ring Stations

Ring stops are upgraded with solar-powered USB charging hubs, directly benefiting the daily student commute.
